Hero frame photos
=================
Drop background-free PNGs here, then set `image` on the matching variant in
  src/features/home/components/Hero3D/index.tsx
e.g.  image: "/images/hero/frames/aviator-bronze.png"

Recommended: ~1400x720, transparent background, product centred,
shot or cut out at a slight 3/4 angle to match the hero's tilt.
